📑 Column meanings

OBJECTID

An internal identifier automatically created by the GIS software (ArcGIS / GeoDatabase).

It’s just a sequential ID, not part of the official Natura 2000 data model.

SITE_CODE

The official EU code for the site, always starts with the country code (e.g. ES for Spain).

Format: ESxxxxxxx (7 digits/letters).

This is the unique identifier across Europe, and you can use it to cross-reference the site in the European Natura 2000 database
SITE_NAME

The official name of the protected site (in the regional language or Spanish).

Examples: Illas Cíes, Fragas do Eume, Costa da Morte.

AC

The Autonomous Community (Comunidad Autónoma) where the site is located.

In your sample: GALICIA.

If a site spans more than one region, sometimes it may appear split by region.

TIPO

The type of Natura 2000 designation.

Codes:

B → LIC/ZEC (Habitats Directive: “Lugar de Importancia Comunitaria” → later “Zona de Especial Conservación”).

C → ZEPA (Birds Directive: “Zona de Especial Protección para las Aves”).

Sometimes you may find other combinations (A, D, etc.), depending on whether it’s both ZEPA and LIC/ZEC.

In your data:

B = Habitat sites

C = Bird sites

HECTAREAS

The area of the site in hectares.

Example: 996.14 ha = ~10 km².

Shape_Leng

The perimeter length of the polygon, in the units of the shapefile’s projection (likely meters).

Example: 22175.31 ≈ 22 km of perimeter.

Shape_Area

The area of the polygon, in square meters (because shapefiles with projected CRS usually store area in m²).

Notice it matches with HECTAREAS × 10,000 (since 1 ha = 10,000 m²).

Example: 9961488.2 m² ≈ 996.15 ha.
